    Geopolitical watch

    Loading recent shipping headlines from GDELT…
    Safety boundary: Unverified news affects risk scores, not hard closures. Closure toggles are simulations. For a real voyage, verify with official maritime warnings, nautical charts, canal authorities and the ship's master.
    ⓘ
    Prototype boundaries. The optimizer searches a 22-port planning network whose lanes follow the Eurostat MARNET maritime network (not charted, collision- or grounding-safe paths). Fuel burn comes from a regression trained on simulated data unless a ship has been calibrated on its noon reports; risk scores are transparent assumptions, not calibrated probabilities. Distances and ETAs are modeled from waypoint segments. Live AIS positions (with a key) and the OFAC sanctions list are connected; verified port dues, contractual fuel rates, berth availability, regulatory clearances and official security notices are not. Dispatch messages are drafts; nothing is sent or booked automatically. The Open-Meteo API provides model-generated marine conditions, not shipboard measurements. Press articles are indicators only.
